Colors of the Rainbow

Welcome to my website about colors. This site explores a collection of colorful images, including red, orange, yellow, green, blue, indigo, purple, pink, and white. Each color has its own unique appearance and can create a different feeling or mood.

Colors are an important part of everyday life. They can be found in nature, art, clothing, design, and many other places. This gallery presents different colors in a simple and responsive layout that can be viewed on different screen sizes.
